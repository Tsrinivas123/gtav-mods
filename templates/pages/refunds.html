Refund Policy

Clear guidelines on refund eligibility and customer support for digital asset purchases.

1. Digital Products Nature

Because PawanMod offers non-tangible, irrevocable digital goods (downloadable vehicle files, map packages, and script archives), orders become non-returnable once files are downloaded or accessed in your dashboard. However, we stand firmly behind the quality of our creators' work and offer full support in resolving technical hurdles.

2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We review and grant refund requests under any of the following circumstances within 7 days of purchase:

  • Corrupted or Non-Functional Asset: The file is proven defective or corrupted, and our technical staff or creator cannot provide a working fix within 48 hours.
  • Duplicate Purchase: You accidentally purchased the identical product more than once in the same transaction or within a 24-hour window.
  • Material Discrepancy: The product received differs significantly from its description and demonstration media on the store page.

3. Non-Eligible Cases

Refunds cannot be issued if:

  • Your hardware or PC does not meet the minimum GTA V system requirements stated on the store page.
  • You simply changed your mind or no longer play GTA V / FiveM after downloading the product.
  • Your account was banned or restricted by third-party server administrators due to rule violations.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, submit a ticket through our Contact Support page or email support@pawanmod.com with your Order Code and detailed explanation. Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
