Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How PawanMod collects, safeguards, and handles your personal information when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, make a purchase, or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect necessary account data such as your username, email address, transaction identifiers, and purchase history. We do not store raw credit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers; payments are processed securely through certified PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Razorpay.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide access to purchased mod downloads and license updates.
  • Send order receipts, security alerts, and customer support ticket replies.
  • Deliver newsletter updates and release announcements (only if you opted in).
  • Prevent fraud and ensure compliance with our platform rules.

3. Cookies & Session Storage

We use essential cookies and session tokens to keep you logged in, preserve your shopping cart across pages, and safeguard against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We only communicate with service providers necessary for infrastructure operation (such as cloud database providers and payment processors).

5. Managing Your Information

You can request data export or account closure at any time by reaching out to our moderators at Contact Support.
