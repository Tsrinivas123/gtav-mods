Purchasing & Instant Downloads

How do I receive my purchased mods?

Purchases are processed instantly. As soon as your checkout payment is confirmed, your download links and license files immediately become available inside your account dashboard under My Orders & Inventory.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept all major Indian and international payment methods via Razorpay, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and digital wallets.

Installation & Compatibility

Are these mods compatible with FiveM and Single Player?

Yes. Every product card lists its compatibility specifications. Most of our vehicle packs and scripts come with both FiveM ready resource folders and OpenIV single-player add-on packages.

Are the mod files safe and scanned for viruses?

100% safe. Each file upload is rigorously audited and scanned with multi-engine antivirus tools before release to ensure no malicious code, trojans, or cryptominers are present.

Updates & Support

Do I get free updates when GTA V updates?

Yes. Whenever Rockstar updates GTA V, our creators patch their mods. All customers receive lifetime access to future compatibility revisions of their purchased items at no extra cost.
